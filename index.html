Our Club

Rooted in Chinhoyi

Redmades FC represents Chinhoyi on the pitch — a club built by the community it plays for, competing week in and week out for local pride.

Led on the pitch by captain Mutonhori, the first team is backed by a growing U19 side currently preparing to enter the Social League.
